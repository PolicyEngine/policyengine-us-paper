% --- Results placeholder ---
% Every empirical number enters the manuscript through a generated table, a
% cited existing artifact (docs, dashboards, release dossiers), or an explicit
% replacement of one of these. It renders loudly so a draft cannot ship with a
% silent gap, and grep-ably ("TODO") so none survives to submission.
\newcommand{\todo}[1]{\textbf{[TODO: #1]}}

% --- Evidence-source annotation ---
% Marks a claim's evidence source inline during drafting (file path, URL, or
% "TODO(evidence needed)"). Intended to be grep-able and removed before
% submission once every claim has been replaced by a citation or a generated
% table reference.
\newcommand{\evidence}[1]{\textcolor{gray}{\footnotesize [evidence: #1]}}
